### Phase A — env difficulty, canonical CQL α=20 recipe

| Env | zs | 0-75k | 75-150k | 150-225k | **225-300k** | peak | Δ vs zs |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| canonical (paddle -30%, warp 0.075) ref | 48 | 72 | 86 | 86 | **83** [65, 102] | 150 @ 146k | +35 |
| `mild_p00` (paddle 0%, warp 0.075) | 58 | 87 | 101 | 97 | **95** [76, 116] | 157 @ 166k | **+37** ⭐ |
| `mild_p10` (paddle -10%, warp 0.075) | 49 | 81 | 86 | 93 | **85** [67, 103] | 140 @ 206k | +36 |
| `hard_w10` (paddle -30%, warp 0.10) | ~33 | 45 | 53 | 51 | **49** [38, 60] | 89 @ 52k | +16 (recipe weakened) |

**Phase A insights**: Recipe transfers cleanly across paddle reductions (Δ ≈ +35–37
across mild_p00, mild_p10, canonical). Warp magnitude is the binding constraint:
warp ≤ 0.075 → recipe works; warp 0.10 → half-strength; warp 0.125 → recipe stalls.
The warp axis is where the source policy's perception is too distorted for the
residual head to learn within 300k.

### Phase B — CQL hyperparameter grid on canonical env (sorted by 225-300k mean)

| Variant | 0-75k | 75-150k | 150-225k | **225-300k** | peak | Δ vs canonical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| **`actor_updates=2`** | 75 | 88 | 95 | **93** [73, 113] | 144 @ 227k | **+10** ⭐⭐ |
| `q_updates=4` | 56 | 62 | 89 | **89** [71, 110] | 145 @ 198k | **+6** ⭐ slow ramp, catches up |
| `α=5` | 74 | 91 | 90 | **87** [68, 111] | 142 @ 254k | +4 |
| canonical (α=20, q=1) ref | 72 | 86 | 86 | **83** [65, 102] | 150 | 0 |
| `α=10` | 68 | 80 | 92 | **78** [61, 100] | 141 @ 176k | -5 (drift) |
| `α=40` | 67 | 79 | 84 | **74** [55, 91] | 142 @ 176k | -9 (over-conservative) |
| `q_updates=8` | 56 | 69 | 75 | **74** [58, 90] | 122 @ 201k | -9 (slow, doesn't catch up) |
| `q_updates=2` | 67 | 86 | 78 | **64** [50, 81] | 163 @ 155k | -19 (drift!) |
| `α=2` | 58 | 60 | 58 | **58** [46, 73] | 90 | -25 (under-conservative) |

**Phase B insights**:
1. **`actor_updates_per_iteration=2` is the strongest single-knob improvement**: +10 over canonical at 300k.
2. **`q_updates=4` is second-best** (+6); slow ramp 0-150k but catches up. Surprising — paddle50-era data said q=4 collapses, but with CQL anchoring Q the higher UTD now works.
3. **q_updates=2 has anomalous drift** (peak 86 in 75-150k → 64 by 225-300k); might be single-seed bad luck or a real "intermediate UTD is unstable" effect.
4. **α=5 marginally beats canonical α=20** (87 vs 83), but well within seed noise.
5. **α=2 under-anchors** (residual head learns nothing); α=40 over-anchors (drift after peak).
6. Sweet zone for α: ≈ 5–20.

### Phase C insights

1. **actor_updates=2 confirms +2 mean / +14 peak over canonical at 1M**.
   Earlier-window advantage is bigger (~+10 at 300k); at 1M the absolute mean
   advantage shrinks but consistency improves. Recipe converges faster.
2. **actor_updates=4 has the best stability** (band lower edge sustained at 79).
   Diminishing returns past actor=2 in mean terms.
3. **Stacking actor=2 + q=4 BACKFIRES** — back-half mean drops 19 below
   canonical. Two improvements interfere; actor over-runs slow-ramping critic.
4. **Cross-env on warp 0.10 (env_hard_w10) stalls at 44** even with actor=2.
   The recipe doesn't transfer to harder envs without further help.

### Phase D insights

1. **Recipe (actor=2) generalizes BETTER on milder envs**: env_mild_p10 reaches
   end mean **117** [94, 142] — zs+68 sustained, the strongest result of the
   campaign. Trajectory is still RISING at end of training (105 → 117).
2. **env_mild_p00 also strong** — end mean 105 (zs+47), peak 175.
3. **Harder env warp 0.10 needs actor=4** — Phase-C actor=2 stalled at 44, but
   Phase-D **actor=4 rescues to 59** (+15 over actor=2). Higher actor updates
   compensate for harder learning signal.
4. **Hardest env warp 0.125 is intractable** within 1M with any recipe variation
   tested; mean stays at 33–35 (~zs+9). This is a structural env limit.

## Conclusion

The two-axis sweep delivered a meaningful recipe upgrade:

- **Mean above zs at 1M (single seed):**
  - Canonical recipe (actor=1): +47 to +49 across mild/canonical envs
  - **NEW recipe (actor=2): +47 to +68 across mild/canonical envs**
  - On warp 0.10: actor=4 gives +26 (vs canonical/actor=2 stalled at +11–16)

- **Faster convergence**: actor=2 reaches the canonical 1M ceiling (~95) by step 400-600k. For the budget-constrained sim2real handoff, this is a 200-400k speedup.

- **Recipe boundary mapped**: works through warp 0.10 with actor=4; fails at warp 0.125.
