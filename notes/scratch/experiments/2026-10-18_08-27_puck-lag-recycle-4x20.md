## Setup

This is `scripts/td3/sim2sim_online_finetune.py --worker --recycle`, the Fig. 6 data-recycling experiment of As et al.

- **Per seed:** 4 trials of 20 online episodes each.
- **Each trial restarts from the source checkpoint**: actor, critics, targets and Adam state.
- **Trial t ≥ 1 samples every batch as (1 − α)·Unif(D0) + α·Unif(D_online).** D0 is the saved online buffers of trials < t.
- **α rises linearly from 0.5 to 1 over the 20 rounds** (`--recycle-anneal-episodes 20` instead of the default 100, to match the trial length).
- **Recipe otherwise unchanged:** the real-robot args file, no warm start.
- **Evaluation:** a deterministic 10-episode eval after every round, on fixed seeds identical across trials, rounds and seeds.

```
.venv/bin/python -u -m scripts.td3.sim2sim_online_finetune --worker --recycle --root runs/td3/sysid_v2_real_workspace/puck_lagcam_online_finetune \
  --env 1 --seed S --learner-device cuda:G --num-online-episodes 20 --eval-episodes 10 --online-eval-every 1 --online-eval-episodes 10 \
  --min-episode-steps 1 --warm-start-episodes 0 --recycle-trials 4 --recycle-alpha-start 0.5 --recycle-anneal-episodes 20 \
  --source-checkpoint 'runs/td3/sysid_v2_real_workspace/hist4_seed{seed}/juggle_sysid_v2_hist4/checkpoint_1975000/training_state.pth'
.venv/bin/python scripts/analysis/aggregate_online_tb.py <root>/online_recycle_alpha0.5/trial{0,1,2,3}
```

## Conclusion

- **Recycling softens the damage but does not fix it.** Trials 1–3 end about 10 below the source policy, against −20 without recycling, and the online return rises from 40 to 50 across trials.
- **No trial improves on the unchanged sim policy**, and everything stays far below the lag-free ~109.
- **With ~5k transitions and 20-episode trials, the recipe cannot learn to compensate for a 120–150 ms observation lag.** It is better addressed in the sim, by training with `puck_obs_lag_seconds`, or on the robot, by fixing the camera buffering.
- **Confidence.** Single seed set. Per-trial differences between trials 1–3 are within the SE.
