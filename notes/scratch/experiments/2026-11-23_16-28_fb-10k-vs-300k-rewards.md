# Controlled comparison of rewards with 10k and 300k inference samples

- **Date**: 2026-11-23 16-28 UTC
- **Status**: done
- **Run dirs**: `runs/fb/reward_inference_300k_20261123`

## Question

Does increasing reward-z inference samples improve task returns at the same checkpoint?

## Setup

Full checkpoint 29,621,664 steps; current-only 29,966,976. Compared each model against itself. Reused saved 300k inference results; inferred new latents from the first 10k transitions of the exact same sampled dataset (seed 200000, uniform with replacement). Evaluated with identical standard episode seeds 100000–100009, deterministic actor actions, and unchanged environment/config. All three tasks; training remained stopped. One inference draw per size, 10 evaluation episodes each.

## Results

Episode return mean ± standard deviation (not standard error):

| Model | Task | 10k | 300k | Mean difference |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| full | juggle | 61.20 ± 34.88 | 65.30 ± 40.68 | +4.10 |
| full | touch | 3.20 ± 2.36 | 4.20 ± 2.48 | +1.00 |
| full | reach | 9.67 ± 5.77 | 12.10 ± 9.02 | +2.43 |
| current | juggle | 19.10 ± 8.24 | 22.20 ± 13.36 | +3.10 |
| current | touch | 0.70 ± 1.27 | 0.20 ± 0.60 | -0.50 |
| current | reach | 1.19 ± 1.72 | 1.19 ± 1.72 | +0.00 |

Full-model reach success decreased from 80% to 60% despite higher accumulated reach reward. Current-only touch success decreased from 30% to 10%; its reach success stayed 20%. Full touch success stayed 100%. Juggle success full: 70%→70%; current: 0%→10%.

## Conclusion

300k improved mean full-model returns in all three tasks and current-model juggle return. Current touch worsened and reach was effectively unchanged. More stable z estimation does not guarantee a better return or success rate. This small paired-seed comparison is descriptive, not evidence of a statistically reliable improvement: only one inference sample draw and 10 episodes per condition.
