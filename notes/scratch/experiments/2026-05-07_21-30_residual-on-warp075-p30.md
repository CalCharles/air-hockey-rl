## Findings

1. **CQL α=20 with no exploration is the clear winner**: end-window mean 83
   ([+35 above zs](mean), band lower edge 65 ([+17 above zs](sustained)),
   peak 150 (3.1× zs). Trajectory ramps to mean 86 by 75-150k and **holds**
   through 300k. This is a categorical improvement over our best paddle50
   result (CQL α=20 there gave 1M back-half mean 68, only +0.5 above zs).
2. **Raw recipe stays at zs**: no drift collapse like paddle50 (which went
   peak 110 → 37 by 1M), but also no improvement — the residual head learns
   nothing useful without anchoring against Q noise.
3. **Exploration hurts at this 300k budget**: both `lite` and `full` primitives
   leave the policy at ≈zs by end of training. The 75-150k window of `lite`
   shows mean 61 (modest improvement) but it doesn't sustain — the policy
   falls back. Hypothesis: primitives drive trajectories off the source-action
   distribution; the residual head can't recover within budget. CQL alone +
   ε=0.05 Gaussian noise stays close to source and finds improvement.

## Conclusion

**On `warp075_p30`, the canonical residual recipe is CQL α=20 with no primitive
exploration.** This single-seed result is dramatically better than anything we
saw on paddle50, and it holds across the back-half of training without drift.

The "exploration helps the residual learn under perturbation" story from the
broken-Polyak / paddle50 era does NOT carry over here — exploration is
strictly harmful at 300k. We should expect this finding to flip if budget is
extended to 1M+, but for the budget the user cares about (300k push-through),
CQL-alone is the recipe.

```
redesign_cql_lite_bc1           67        64         64         112      −19 (vs lite alone +12)
```

### Round 2 findings

- **BC stacking on top of CQL hurts monotonically** (λ=0.5 → −10, λ=1.0 → −13).
  CQL alone already provides sufficient anchoring; BC's drag-to-zero just suppresses
  residual learning.
- **N=10 hurts back-half** (−11 in 225-300k). Larger ensemble = slower learning =
  drift signature in the final window at 300k budget. Same effect we saw on
  paddle50 at 1M. N=5 with CQL is the right ensemble size.
- **BC partially rescues exploration** (lite+BC=64 vs lite-alone=52, +12) but the
  rescued cell still loses to CQL-alone by −19. Exploration is the wrong axis here.
